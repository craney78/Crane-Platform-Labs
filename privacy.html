Privacy policy.

How we handle personal information across our business website and related enquiries.

Updated 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Crane Platform Labs is an Australian website and digital-platform business. We develop products including Find Similar Charts and EdgeBreak. Their individual websites may contain more detailed, product-specific privacy terms.

Information we may receive

If you email or call us, we may receive your contact details, enquiry and any information you choose to provide. When visitors use our website, ordinary hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and diagnostic logs.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide services, protect our systems, understand technical issues and meet legal requirements. We may use service providers for hosting, communications, analytics and other operational needs.

Third-party products and links

Our website links to separate product websites including Find Similar Charts and EdgeBreak. Those services may use authentication, subscriptions, payment processing, market data and AI providers. Please review the privacy terms on the relevant product website for more information.

Retention and safeguards

We aim to keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, operational, legal and security purposes, and use reasonable protective measures. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about access, correction or privacy concerns. Some records may need to be kept to satisfy legal or operational obligations.

Overseas services

Some technology providers we use may process or store information outside Australia. Their relevant arrangements and privacy practices apply.

Contact

For privacy enquiries email info@craneplatformlabs.com.au.
