Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A few straightforward answers about the studio, what we build and our own products.

What does Crane Platform Labs build?

We build professional websites and specialist web platforms such as membership experiences, customer tools, dashboards and AI-assisted interfaces.

Do you build websites for other businesses?

Yes. We consider business websites and selected web-platform projects. Start with a short enquiry describing what you need.

What are your own products?

Our two financial-technology products are Find Similar Charts and EdgeBreak. Learn more on Our Platforms.

Is Find Similar Charts a stock-tipping service?

No. It is designed for technical chart research, including chart-pattern discovery and AI-assisted chart interpretation, not personalised investment advice or trade execution.

Can you quote a project immediately?

We need to understand the scope first. Requirements, integrations and ongoing maintenance affect cost. We prefer clear project discussions over unrealistic instant quotes.

Where are you based?

Crane Platform Labs is based in regional New South Wales, Australia, and works on digital products and websites for a broader audience.

How do I get product support?

Visit our support page to find the appropriate email contact for each product.
