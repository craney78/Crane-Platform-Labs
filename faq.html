Why isn’t my business showing up on Google?

Businesses often struggle to appear on Google because they have weak local SEO, incomplete Google Business Profiles, poor website structure or limited local relevance.

Improving your Google Business Profile , website SEO and local content can help improve visibility in Google Search and Google Maps.

Why is my website not generating enquiries?

Many websites fail to generate enquiries because they are difficult to use, poorly optimised for mobile phones, slow to load or unclear about their services.

A good local business website should clearly explain services, build trust and make it easy for customers to contact your business.

Why do mobile-friendly websites rank better on Google?

Google prioritises mobile-friendly websites because most searches now happen on phones and tablets.

Websites that are difficult to use on mobile devices often lose visitors quickly and may perform worse in search rankings.
