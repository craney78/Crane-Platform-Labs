Find Similar Charts: from chart research to AI-powered tools

We built a focused US stock-chart research website around a simple idea: start with a chart pattern, not a complicated scanner. Here is what exists now—and what we are developing next.

Crane Platform Labs · 6 November 2026 · First-party project case study

Find Similar Charts is a web-based research product created by Crane Platform Labs. It lets users supply a chart screenshot, make their own drawing or choose a reference pattern, then search supported NASDAQ and NYSE stocks for comparable technical structures. Its separate AI Chart Analyzer describes technical features in charts supplied by customers.

The problem we wanted to solve

Traditional stock scanners can be powerful, but filtering for a visual setup often involves knowing indicator definitions and combining many technical settings. Sometimes a trader already knows the shape they are looking for: a tightening base, an ascending triangle or another recognisable structure.

We wanted to make that starting point more natural. Instead of building a long filter query, users can show the system a visual reference and examine candidates returned by the matching workflow. Matches are research leads, not claims that two charts are identical or that a particular price move will follow.

What we've built for the website

  • Find Similar Charts: similarity searches from customer-uploaded screenshots, drawings and selected reference patterns, returning candidate charts for comparison.
  • AI Chart Analyzer: interpretation of a customer's uploaded or drawn stock chart, helping explain visible structure and technical signals without guaranteeing an outcome.
  • End-of-day technical chart tools: supported US ticker charts and indicator information designed for research, not live trade execution.
  • Stock Hub and personal watchlists: research and follow-up workflows available according to the current website's access rules.
  • Daily chart discoveries: a workspace view of ranked charts from completed NASDAQ and NYSE scanning sessions, subject to available published results and membership limits.

There is one important separation in the product: library setup images are search references only. The AI Chart Analyzer is for charts supplied by the customer, not for analysing the preset library illustrations. Access levels and current inclusions are shown on the product's membership page.

How EdgeBreak helped us build it

Find Similar Charts builds on the market-research and charting knowledge developed during our earlier EdgeBreak work. This provided a starting point for technical data, end-of-day charts and pattern-research workflows while allowing us to design a simpler customer experience.

The two brands have different purposes. EdgeBreak is the wider scanning and stock-research platform; Find Similar Charts concentrates on visual chart discovery and analysis. We aim to keep existing infrastructure and customer access boundaries clear rather than treating the two websites as interchangeable.

What we're building next

We are working on ways to make the same core research workflow accessible beyond the website. These are development projects, not completed public releases:

  • ChatGPT integration — in development. A dedicated Find Similar Charts ChatGPT app is being developed to provide conversational access to similar-chart searches, chart analysis and supported research tools. It has not yet been verified as accepted or publicly available inside ChatGPT.
  • Android app / Google Play — in development. Work is underway towards a mobile experience. We are not claiming a public Google Play listing or an approved release.
  • iPhone app / Apple App Store — in development. Work is underway towards an iOS experience. We are not claiming an App Store listing or an approved release.

We will link to official app-store listings only when they actually exist. Development progress is not the same as release approval, and no launch date is being promised here.

What this demonstrates about our work

For Crane Platform Labs, this project is a practical example of taking a technical idea through interface design, data integration, chart visualisation, AI-assisted workflows and an ongoing product roadmap. It also shows why careful data provenance, entitlement rules and failure handling matter in customer-facing software.

Visitors can inspect the public website and judge the design and workflows themselves. They should not treat our case study as an independent review or as evidence of a verified investment outcome.
