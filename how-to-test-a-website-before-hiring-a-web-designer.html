GOOGLE PAGESPEED INSIGHTS

Test Website Speed, Mobile Usability & SEO Performance

Google PageSpeed Insights is one of the easiest ways to quickly test how a business website performs across mobile devices and desktop browsers.

Website speed and usability now play an important role in customer experience, Google rankings and how effectively a website generates enquiries online. Slow-loading websites, oversized images and poor mobile layouts can negatively affect both SEO and customer engagement.

Most business website traffic now comes from mobile devices, which means poor mobile usability can directly affect customer enquiries, accessibility and long-term Google visibility.

Common website problems may include:

Large uncompressed images
Slow-loading homepage banners
Cluttered mobile layouts
Hard-to-find contact information
Poor accessibility contrast
Excessive animations and scripts

Google PageSpeed Insights helps test:

Website loading speed
Mobile responsiveness
Accessibility standards
Local SEO and Google visibility
Website best practices
Core Web Vitals performance
0–49 Poor
50–89 Needs Improvement
90–100 Good

A perfect score is not always necessary — however consistently low scores can indicate performance, usability or accessibility issues that may negatively affect customer experience and Google visibility.

Website testing tools should be used as a guide rather than the only measure of website quality. Good design, strong customer experience and clear enquiry pathways remain equally important.
