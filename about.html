About

We build useful digital systems, then make them feel simple.

Crane Platform Labs is an independent Australian technology and digital-product studio. Our focus is practical software, dependable data and customer experiences people can understand without a manual.

Why Crane Platform Labs exists

Good digital products should remove work, not create more of it. We build products by starting with a real workflow, understanding what information matters, and reducing everything else until the experience feels clear.

Our flagship product, EdgeBreak, applies that approach to NASDAQ scanner research: strong underlying data, visible freshness and a guided journey that still lets customers ask naturally for what they want.
