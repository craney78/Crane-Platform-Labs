About the studio

We build practical things for the web.

Crane Platform Labs is an independent Australian website and digital-platform business. We care about clear websites, dependable systems and making complicated workflows easier to use.

What we're building

Our work spans business websites, custom online platforms and our own software products. We started with a focus on practical website building and expanded into research systems, charting tools and AI-assisted interfaces.

Two of our products demonstrate that work: Find Similar Charts, our current chart-focused product under development, and EdgeBreak, the established market-research platform and underlying technology we've developed.

We're based in regional New South Wales and build for customers who value straightforward solutions over unnecessary complexity.

Our focus

Build something useful. Then make it easier to use.

We want websites to communicate clearly and platforms to solve problems for actual customers.
