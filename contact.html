What to include

You don't need a polished specification. A few practical details are enough to start a conversation.

  • Is it a business website, a customer platform or another digital tool?
  • What do you want the customer to be able to do?
  • Do you already have a website or something in progress?
  • Is there a preferred timeframe or rough budget?
We take on projects selectively. We'll discuss scope and suitability before making any commitments about costs or delivery.
