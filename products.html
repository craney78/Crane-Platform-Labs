EdgeBreak Flagship

NASDAQ scanner research built for exploration.

EdgeBreak brings published scanner results, Pressure Building Index context, technical charts, signal history and watchlist workflows into one focused research experience.

  • Breakout and Pre-Breakout scanner exploration
  • PBI score and ranking context
  • Technical charts, scanner context and signal history
  • Filtering, comparison and watchlist workflows
  • Natural-language interaction alongside guided controls
EdgeBreak provides market research and informational tools. It does not execute trades and does not provide personalised investment advice.
