Find Similar Charts

A dedicated chart-matching and AI-chart-analysis experience for people researching technical setups in US stocks.

Find Similar Charts is designed around a simple starting point: show the platform the chart structure you're interested in, then explore similar patterns. AI chart analysis is a separate workflow for customer-supplied charts.

  • Visual searches for similar stock chart structures
  • AI analysis of customer-provided chart images
  • Customer-focused design that reduces complex scanner steps
