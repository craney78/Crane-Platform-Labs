Our work / Platform case study

EdgeBreak: stock scanners, market research and ChatGPT integration

A working product is better evidence than a list of promises. Here is what Crane Platform Labs developed, how the pieces fit together and where you can inspect the public-facing product.

Crane Platform Labs · 6 November 2026 · First-party project case study

EdgeBreak is a stock-market research platform developed by Crane Platform Labs. Its customer-facing website brings together scheduled US market scans, technical charts, research tools and a conversational workflow, including an integration with ChatGPT. Rather than presenting an imaginary portfolio example, we link to the actual platform so visitors can examine the experience for themselves.

The problem: research spread across too many steps

Looking for a technical setup can mean moving between a scanner, a chart, indicator calculations, historical signals and company information. A list of stock symbols alone does not answer why a candidate appeared or what data produced the result. We built EdgeBreak to connect those steps while keeping the customer's decisions in their own hands.

The aim is research and explanation, not stock recommendations or automated trading. The product helps people investigate published market information; it does not predict returns or place trades.

What we developed

  • Daily NASDAQ and NYSE scanning: published post-session results for supported setups such as Breakouts, Pre-Breakouts and bases, with dates and scan context so visitors know which trading session they are reviewing.
  • A flexible Master Scanner: filters for price structure, support, resistance, higher lows, moving averages and available participation measures, including PBI, OBV and relative volume (RVOL).
  • First-party stock charts: chart views connected to scanner evidence, technical indicators and available signal history, rather than a disconnected list of tickers.
  • Stock Hub and company research: ticker-led workflows joining chart structure with available company information, financial context, news and filings.
  • AI chart workflows and watchlists: tools for describing customer-supplied chart screenshots, searching for comparable chart structures and returning to stocks worth further investigation.

Not every capability is available on every plan, and results depend on data coverage and completed scan sessions. The EdgeBreak website is the source for current features, access and product demonstrations.

Connecting market research to ChatGPT

One part of the project was creating an EdgeBreak integration for ChatGPT. Rather than asking a customer to learn specialised scanner commands, the integration is designed to let them describe a setup in ordinary language, refine published scanner results, inspect charts and continue stock research in a conversation.

It is an integration with ChatGPT, not a claim that Crane Platform Labs developed ChatGPT or has an OpenAI partnership. EdgeBreak describes access for eligible Pro members, with tool availability depending on the supported integration capabilities. Its ChatGPT experience has documented NASDAQ research workflows; NYSE coverage inside ChatGPT has not been verified, even though the EdgeBreak website supports both exchanges.

The engineering behind the visible screens

The website is the visible part of a larger system. Scans must run against supported market data, produce a dated publication and expose consistent results to chart, search and research interfaces. A useful platform also needs to distinguish an end-of-day scan from a current price and an older historical signal.

We developed scheduled scanning and publication workflows, technical-data processing, chart rendering, searchable research context and customer-facing interfaces around that distinction. Account access, data freshness, incomplete results and failure states matter just as much as the headline features. Those are ongoing operational responsibilities, not evidence that every service is error-free.

What this work proves about Crane Platform Labs

EdgeBreak demonstrates that our studio has built more than a brochure website: a multi-part digital product with proprietary research logic, connected data workflows, user interfaces and conversational AI access. Someone considering us for a data-heavy platform, customer portal or specialised AI integration can follow the links and judge the product directly.

It does not prove trading performance, guarantee uptime or imply that every feature can be used without an account. Those claims would need separate evidence.

How it led to Find Similar Charts

The research and charting work behind EdgeBreak also informed Find Similar Charts, our more focused chart-led product. That product centres on showing a reference setup and exploring similar US stock chart structures, rather than requiring customers to build complex scanner filters first. Both products are examples of software developed by Crane Platform Labs.
